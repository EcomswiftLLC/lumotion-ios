Make a still photo move.

Lumotion turns one photo into a seamless loop, and one short video into a cinemagraph. Paint where the motion goes. It is free, there is no account, and everything is made on your device.

Motion that you direct

Flow brush
Paint the water, the smoke or the hair, then draw arrows to say which way it moves. Set the speed and the strength. Add as many regions as you like, each with its own settings. The loop is seamless.
Anchor brush
Pin the parts that must stay still, so a river can run past a bridge that does not budge.
Cinemagraph from video
Choose a clip, choose a loop of up to six seconds, choose the still frame, and paint the part that keeps moving. The loop is smoothed at the ends, or plays back and forth.
3D parallax and depth zoom
Separate a subject from its background and move the camera between them. Depth can come from the subject Lumotion finds on your device, from depth saved in a portrait photo, or from a brush you paint yourself. Revealed edges are filled so there are no holes.
Camera motion
Zoom in or out, pan, drift, rotate, add a handheld shake, or let the frame breathe, with easing you can choose.
Living effects for the whole photo
Eleven effects: water ripple, waves, wind sway, heat haze, shimmer, pulse, light sweep, glitch, wobble, wiggle and ink swirl, over the whole picture or just the part you choose.
Overlays
Rain, heavy rain, snow, blizzard, falling leaves, petals, fireflies, sparkles, bokeh, dust, embers, smoke and fog, lens flare, light leaks, twinkling stars, confetti, hearts, bubbles, shooting stars and fireworks. Stack several, and set the intensity, speed, size and tint of each.
Looks, text and stickers
24 looks, adjustment dials and animated film grain, plus text and emoji stickers with entrance and exit animations.
Magic presets
18 one-tap starting points, such as Waterfall, Clouds drift, Rainy window or Parallax portrait. They are starting points, so you can change everything afterwards.
Undo and redo, for everything
Compare with the original, step back through your edits, and reorder or remove layers. Your project is saved as you go.

Ready for wherever it is going

Pick the shape first: the original, square, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 or 3:4, and reframe with a gesture. Choose a loop length from 1 to 15 seconds and a frame rate, then export.

  • MP4 video, in H.264 or HEVC, at 720p and 1080p, plus 2K and 4K on devices with enough memory
  • GIF, with a size estimate before you export
  • A still frame as an image
  • Save to Photos, share, or open in Files
  • No watermark

You can add your own audio file to a video, with trim, volume and fade. Lumotion does not include music.

Your photos never leave your device

Lumotion has no account and no server that stores your pictures. Every frame is made on your phone or iPad. We do not run analytics of our own.

  • You pick photos and videos with the iOS photo picker, so Lumotion sees only what you choose
  • Saving to your library uses add-only permission
  • Subject finding for depth effects runs on the device
  • Projects are saved on your device and can be deleted from the gallery
  • No account, no sign-in, no subscription

The only outside service is the ad network. The privacy policy explains what it collects.

Free, and paid for by ads

Lumotion has no subscription and no in-app purchase. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it, and they appear in two places.

Where you will see ads

  • A banner on the Home screen. Never over the editing controls.
  • One short rewarded video before you export. You choose whether to watch it, and one watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes.

When no ad is available

  • The export goes ahead for free.
  • This happens, for example, when you are offline.

Where the law asks for it, the app shows a consent form from Google before personalised ads, and iOS may ask whether the app may track you. Lumotion itself runs no analytics, and the only tracking that can happen is through Google's ad software, and only if you allow it. You can say no to the iOS prompt and still use everything. Settings has an Ad privacy choices row where one is required.

Questions

Is Lumotion really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, no paid version and no watermark. Ads from Google AdMob pay for the app: a banner on the Home screen, and one rewarded video that you choose to watch before you export.

Are my photos and videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Animating, previewing and exporting all happen on your device. We never receive your photos, videos or audio, and nothing is sent to an AI service.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no sign-up.

What is a cinemagraph?

A cinemagraph is a short looping clip in which only part of the picture moves, such as steam rising from a cup in an otherwise still frame. In Lumotion you pick a clip, choose the loop, choose the still frame, and paint the part that keeps moving. The ends of the loop are blended so it repeats without a jump.

Does Lumotion use AI?

It does not generate pictures and it does not send your photos anywhere. Motion comes from the areas, arrows and settings that you choose. For depth effects, the app can find the main subject using Apple's Vision technology on your device.

Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Before you export, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes, so you can save several versions in a row. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline, the export goes ahead anyway. Editing never needs an ad.

What can I export?

An MP4 video (H.264 or HEVC), a GIF, or a still image. You can save to Photos, share, or open in Files. There is no watermark.

Does it need photo library or camera access?

Not to edit. You pick photos and videos with the iOS photo picker, and Lumotion sees only the ones you choose. To save a result, the app asks for add-only permission, which lets it add items and does not let it read your library. The camera is used only if you choose to take a photo to animate.

Does it come with music?

No. There is no bundled music. You can add an audio file of your own from the Files app, trim it, set the volume and fade it. You are responsible for having the right to use it.

What happens if I do not allow tracking?

The app works the same. The ads you see will be less personalised. You can change the choice any time in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking. Lumotion itself runs no analytics. If you allow tracking, only Google's ad software may use your advertising identifier for ads.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later, in portrait and landscape.

When can I download it?

Lumotion is not on the App Store yet, so there is no download link. This page will link to the App Store listing once the app is available.
