Common questions

How do I make a photo move?
  1. Choose a photo, or one of the sample photos, on the Home screen.
  2. Open Flow in the tool dock and paint over the part that should move, such as water or smoke.
  3. Draw arrows inside the painted area to show the direction. Set the speed and strength.
  4. Use the anchor brush on anything that must stay still.
  5. Press play to preview the loop, then export.

Magic presets give you a starting point in one tap. You can change everything they set.

How do I make a cinemagraph from a video?
  1. Choose a video from your library. Clips that were filmed on a steady surface or tripod work best.
  2. Open Video, and choose the loop: where it starts and ends, up to six seconds.
  3. Choose the still frame, then paint the part that should keep moving. The rest stays frozen.
  4. Raise the loop smoothing if you can see a jump when the clip repeats, or use the back-and-forth loop.
  5. Export it as an MP4 or a GIF.
Why am I asked to watch an ad?

Lumotion is free, and ads pay for it. Before you export, the app offers one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If you choose Not now, nothing is exported and you can try again any time. If no ad is available, for example when you are offline or your ad privacy choice does not allow ads, the export goes ahead without one. Editing never needs an ad.

Which file should I export for Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp?

Export an MP4 and use Share, then pick the app. Choose 9:16 for stories and short videos, 4:5 or 1:1 for feed posts. GIF files are larger and lower in quality than MP4, so use a GIF only when you need one. Lumotion shows a size estimate before a GIF export.

My export is slow or stops

High resolutions, long loops and a high frame rate take more time and memory, especially on older devices. Try a shorter loop, 30 frames per second or a lower resolution. Keep the app open until the export finishes, and make sure your device has free storage.

Where are my projects?

Projects are saved on your device as you work and appear on the Home screen. You can rename, duplicate or delete them there. Lumotion keeps projects only on your device, so if you delete the app, or delete a project, they cannot be recovered by us. Files you exported to Photos or Files are not affected. A full backup of your device may still contain the app's data if you made one before deleting it.

I saw an ad that seems wrong

Write to us at the address above. Tell us roughly when and where in the app you saw it and what it showed. A screenshot helps. We block ad categories and advertisers through Google AdMob where we can.

I want to change my ad or tracking choice

Open Settings in the app and look for the Ad privacy choices row, which appears where the law requires it. You can also change tracking in iOS Settings under Privacy and Security, then Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.
