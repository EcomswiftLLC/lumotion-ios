## 1. What Lumotion is

Lumotion is an app for iPhone and iPad that turns a photo into a looping animation, and turns a short video into a cinemagraph. It lets you animate parts of a picture with a brush, move the camera through a photo, add depth and parallax, add overlays such as rain, snow and sparkles, apply looks, add text and stickers, and export the result as an MP4 video, a GIF or a still image.
