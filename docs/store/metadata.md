## Description (3293 of 4000)

Honest wording: no star or review claims, no competitor names, no "AI", no claim a test cannot prove, and the ad model stated plainly.

```
Make a still photo move. Lumotion turns one photo into a seamless looping animation and one short video into a cinemagraph, all on your iPhone or iPad. Paint where the motion goes, add depth, rain or sparkles, then export an MP4 or a GIF. It is free, there is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

PAINT THE MOTION
- Flow brush: paint an area, draw arrows to set the direction, then set the speed and strength. Water, smoke, clouds and hair can drift in a loop that closes without a seam
- Several flow areas in one photo, each with its own arrows, speed, strength and softness
- Anchor brush: pin parts of the picture so they stay perfectly still
- Eraser, undo and redo, brush size and feather

CINEMAGRAPHS FROM VIDEO
- Choose a clip, choose a loop of up to 6 seconds and a still frame, then paint the part that keeps moving. The rest stays frozen
- Loop smoothing blends the ends so the clip repeats without a jump, or play it back and forth

DEPTH AND CAMERA
- 3D parallax: move the camera between a subject and its background. Depth can come from the subject found on your device, from depth saved in portrait photos, from a depth brush you paint, or from a simple gradient. Revealed edges are filled, so there are no holes
- Camera moves: zoom in or out, pan, drift, rotate, handheld shake and breathe, with the easing you choose

LIVING EFFECTS AND OVERLAYS
- 11 whole-photo effects: water ripple, waves, wind sway, heat haze, shimmer, pulse, light sweep, glitch, wobble, wiggle and ink swirl, over the whole picture or only where you paint
- 20 overlays: rain, heavy rain, snow, blizzard, falling leaves, petals, fireflies, sparkles, bokeh, dust, embers, smoke and fog, lens flare, light leaks, twinkling stars, confetti, hearts, bubbles, shooting stars and fireworks. Stack as many as you like and set the intensity, speed, size and tint of each

MAGIC PRESETS
- 18 one-tap starting points, such as Waterfall, Clouds drift, Rainy window, Neon rain or Parallax portrait. They are starting points, so you can change everything afterwards

LOOKS, TEXT AND SOUND
- 24 looks, adjustment dials and animated film grain
- Text and emoji stickers with entrance and exit animations
- Add an audio file of your own, with trim, volume and fade. Lumotion does not include music

EXPORT YOUR WAY
- MP4 video (H.264 or HEVC) at 720p and 1080p, plus 2K and 4K on devices with enough memory, GIF, or a still image
- Original, square, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and 3:4 shapes, loops from 1 to 15 seconds, 24, 30 or 60 frames per second
- Save to Photos, share, or open in Files. No watermark
- Projects save as you work, so you can come back to them any time

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Every frame is made on your device. Your photos, videos and audio are never uploaded, and there is no account. Lumotion finds the subject for depth effects with Apple's Vision technology on the device.

FREE, WITH ADS
Lumotion is free. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it: a banner on the Home screen and, before you export, one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If no ad is available, your export goes ahead. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the ads may use your advertising identifier. The app works the same either way.

Questions or ideas? Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.
```

## App Review notes (1698 of 4000)

```
Lumotion turns a photo into a looping animation and a short video into a cinemagraph. There is no account, no login and no demo credentials. Everything works on first launch.

How to test:
1. Finish the short welcome screens. On Home, tap one of the sample photos (built into the app), or choose a photo from your library.
2. In the editor, open Presets and tap one, or open Flow, paint over an area and draw an arrow. Press play to see the loop. Overlays, Camera, Depth, Looks and Text are in the tool dock.
3. Tap Export and choose MP4, GIF or still image.

Ads: the app is free and supported by Google AdMob. A banner appears on Home only. Before an export, the app offers one opt-in rewarded video ("Watch a short ad to export"); one watched ad covers exports for 2 minutes. If no ad is available (offline, no fill, consent pending), the export simply goes ahead without one. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no watermark. A Google UMP consent form may appear first in regions where it is required, followed by the App Tracking Transparency prompt; the app works the same whichever choice is made.

Permissions: Photos (read access through the system photo picker, add-only access to save results), Camera (only if the user taps to take a photo to animate), Tracking (App Tracking Transparency, for ads). The app does not use the microphone or location.

Privacy: photos and videos are processed on the device and are never uploaded. The only network traffic is the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Privacy policy: https://ecomswiftllc.github.io/lumotion/privacy/

Sample photos and clips bundled with the app are from Pexels (Pexels License); credits are in the project documentation.
```
